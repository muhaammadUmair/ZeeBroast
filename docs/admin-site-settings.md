# Admin: Site Settings

`Admin → Site Settings` is organized into panels:

## General
Site name, tagline, favicon, logo, currency symbol, order ID prefix (used to generate
order codes like `ZB1A2B3C`).

## Hero Section
The three lines of the homepage's big headline text, plus its subtitle.

## Contact & Social
Phone, email, address (also shown in the footer and Contact page), and social links
(Facebook/Instagram/Twitter).

## Delivery & Ordering
- Delivery Fee (Rs.)
- Free Delivery Above (Rs.) — orders at or above this subtotal get free delivery
- Minimum Order Amount (Rs.)
- Estimated Delivery time (minutes) — shown to customers on confirmation/tracking pages
- "100% Halal Certified" badge toggle

## Brand Colors
Primary/accent color and background color — stored for reference; changing the actual
site-wide palette also requires updating `assets/css/style.css` variables.

## Loyalty / Referral Points
See the [Loyalty Points guide](loyalty-points.md#configuration-admin--site-settings) for
the full breakdown of every setting in this panel.
